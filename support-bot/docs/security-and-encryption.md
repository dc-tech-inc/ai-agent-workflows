# Security and encryption

## Encryption

Files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and travel over TLS 1.3. Fernwood staff cannot open the contents of your files.

## Private key option

You can switch on a private encryption key under Settings, then Security. With a private key only you can decrypt your files, which also means that if you lose the key, we cannot recover your data.

## Two-factor sign-in

Two-factor sign-in works with any authenticator app. When you enable it, you receive 10 single-use recovery codes. Store them somewhere safe, because support cannot disable two-factor for you without verifying your identity in writing.

## Where data is stored

When you create the account you choose a storage region, either the United States or the European Union. Changing the region later requires contacting support and takes about 5 business days.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Security researchers can report issues to the address on our security page. We acknowledge reports within 3 business days.
