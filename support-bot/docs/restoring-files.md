# Restoring files

## Restoring from the web

Sign in to the Fernwood web app, browse to the file or folder and choose Restore. You can also download a whole folder as a zip archive, up to 50 GB per download.

## Restoring an older version

Open the file, choose Versions and pick the date you want. How far back you can go depends on your plan: 7 days on Free, 30 days on Personal, 90 days on Family and 365 days on Business.

## Recovering deleted files

Files you delete on your computer are moved to the Fernwood trash and can be recovered for 30 days. After 30 days they are permanently removed and cannot be restored by support either.

## Restoring to a new computer

Install the desktop app on the new computer, sign in and choose Restore to this computer. Files are downloaded in the background, and the most recently changed files arrive first.
