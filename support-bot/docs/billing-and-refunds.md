# Billing and refunds

## Refund policy

You can request a full refund within 30 days of your first purchase of a paid plan. After 30 days, annual plans are refunded pro rata for the unused full months, and monthly plans are not refunded.

## Invoices

Invoices are available as PDF files under Account, then Billing. Business plan owners can add a company name and a tax number to every invoice.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry it 3 times over 7 days and send an email after each attempt. If the payment still fails, the account moves to the Free plan after 14 days, and files above the 5 GB limit become read-only instead of being deleted.

## Cancelling

You can cancel under Account, then Billing, then Cancel plan. The plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

## Payment methods

We accept major credit and debit cards. Bank transfers are available for annual Business plans only.
